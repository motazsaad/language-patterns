Meet 90

One number, four different ways to build it. French famously does a little arithmetic.

ENGLISH ninety a single word
FRANÇAIS quatre-vingt-dix four twenties and ten (4 × 20 + 10)
ITALIANO novanta the regular word for ninety
العَرَبِيَّة تِسْعُونَ / تِسْعِينَ tisʿūna / tisʿīna — the form changes with grammatical case

↳ In Belgium and parts of Switzerland, French speakers often say nonante instead. In Arabic, تسعون is the nominative form; تسعين is used in the accusative and genitive. In many dialects, تسعين (tisʿīn) is the everyday form.
